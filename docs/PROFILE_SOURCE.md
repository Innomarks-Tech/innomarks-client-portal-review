Innomarks Technology Consulting is a technology and business consulting division operating under Innomarks Group. It helps businesses improve efficiency, adopt innovative technologies, and make informed decisions through practical consulting solutions.

Vision: become South Africa's trusted technology consulting partner, empowering organisations through innovation, digital transformation, and intelligent business solutions.

Mission: help businesses improve efficiency, productivity, security, and growth through practical technology-driven consulting.

## Service areas in the profile

- Technology Consulting: understand business challenges and deliver tailored technology strategy and practical solutions.
- Digital Transformation: digitise processes, optimise workflows, and implement technology that improves efficiency, collaboration, and performance.
- Software Development: build customised software, web, and mobile solutions that automate processes and address business challenges.
- Data Analytics & Business Intelligence: turn data into dashboards, reporting, actionable insights, performance monitoring, and planning support.
- AI & Business Automation: use AI and automation to streamline repetitive tasks, improve decisions, raise productivity, and enable smarter operations.
- IT Governance & Cybersecurity: support governance, cybersecurity awareness, risk management, compliance advisory, and practices that protect business information and technology assets.
